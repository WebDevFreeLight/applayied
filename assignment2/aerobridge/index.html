The system

Four layers, one lifeline.

① Control Tower

Autonomy stack: airspace deconfliction, routing, weather-aware dispatch and live telemetry.

② Vertiport Hubs

Automated loading, cold-chain staging and battery swap. Click a node to highlight.

③ Autonomous Fleet

Redundant eVTOL craft with sealed, monitored medical payload pods.

④ Clinic Endpoints

Precision drop, signature-free custody handoff and automatic inventory sync.

A real use case

From alert to arrival — step through it.

1 · Trigger
