The system

Sense → Twin → Predict → Respond.

① Sense

Edge sensors & existing SCADA feeds unified into a live data fabric.

② Twin

A geospatial digital twin models every asset and its dependencies.

③ Predict

ML forecasts degradation, demand peaks and failure probability.

④ Respond

Coordinated dispatch, routing and cross-agency playbooks.

A real use case

A water main is about to fail. Watch MetroPulse act.

1 · Predict

Pressure anomaly → 86% failure probability within 5 days.

2 · Correlate

Twin flags 3 dependent assets & a school on the line.

3 · Plan

Optimal maintenance window chosen to minimise disruption.

4 · Dispatch

Crew routed around live traffic; valves pre-staged.

5 · Outcome

Repaired pre‑failure. Zero outage.
